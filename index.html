Create your city soundtrack: a social music plan for students

Don't just listen to your city. Create it. Build, vote on and live-listen to City Soundtracks with the people who share your streets.

How it works

Three actions turn a playlist into the sound of a place.

1

Create

Pick a city, a mood and a genre, then start a City Soundtrack that is public, community-run or private.

2

Vote

Anyone can add songs. The community keeps, removes or marks a track Must Play, and the ranking updates.

3

Listen live

Join a synchronized session where everyone hears the same track and chats with SoundChat.

Who it's for

18-40-year-olds in Indian cities, mainly students and young professionals, who want music to be social instead of solo.

Campus soundtracks, city rankings and City vs City battles give every community something to join and share.

What you get, in your words


"I discover songs from people who actually live in my city."

"I can listen live with others and chat about the track."

"My café or college gets its own soundtrack."

More than an app

The soundtrack reaches physical places and creates new ways to earn.

SoundSpace

Cafés, gyms and campuses

Customers scan a QR or NFC point to join a venue's soundtrack, vote and chat.

Sound Creators

Local artists and curators

Artists submit tracks to city soundtracks. Top curators earn through sponsored and premium soundtracks.

City vs City

Battles that spread

Cities compete on votes and participation. The winner becomes City Sound of the Month.
